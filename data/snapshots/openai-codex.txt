Frequently asked questions
What happens when you hit usage limits?
If you are approaching your usage limit, switching to a lower-cost model can help your allowance last longer. GPT-6 Astra usage varies by task; see the usage table for current estimates.
Where can I see my current usage limits?
You can find your current limits in the
Codex usage dashboard
⁠
. If you want to see your remaining limits during an active Codex CLI session, you can use /status.
How do credits work?
Credits let you use Codex beyond the usage included in your plan. Credit usage varies by model and task. GPT-6 Astra has no separate compute-unit charge at launch. Existing models keep their current pricing.
What can I do to make my usage limits last longer?
Switch to a lower-cost model for routine tasks to help your usage limits last longer. Usage varies by model and task; see the usage table for current estimates.
Is GPT-6 Astra included in my plan?
GPT-6 Astra is included within the existing Codex usage allowance on the $100 and $200 Pro plans and the $100 Business plan. Plus and the $20 Business plan include limited GPT-6 Astra usage, with the option to purchase credits for additional access.
